What the calculator tells you

It does not guess a price. Tree work is priced by the job, and a number from a form would ignore your access, the tree's condition and the rules that apply. Instead it shows a job profile, the factors that will shape your quote and the council checks to make first. The tree surgeon then sends a written quote.

  • How involved the job is, from straightforward to specialist
  • What will shape your quote, from tree size to access
  • Whether notice or consent may be needed first
  • A reminder about nesting birds and ash dieback where they apply

How Croydon Tree Surgeon works

We're upfront about this: Croydon Tree Surgeon is a lead generation website. We don't carry out any tree work. When you get in touch, we pass your enquiry to one local, insured tree surgeon who assesses, quotes and completes the job directly with you. The tree surgeon pays us for the enquiries we send; you pay us nothing, and we add nothing to their price. Your details are never sold or passed to several companies.
