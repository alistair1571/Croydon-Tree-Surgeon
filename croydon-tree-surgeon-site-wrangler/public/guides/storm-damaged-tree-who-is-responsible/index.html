First

  1. If anyone is in danger, call 999.
  2. Keep away from the tree and anything it touches. If power lines are down, call 105.
  3. Take photos from a safe distance, with the date and time.

Who is responsible?

A tree belongs to the owner of the land where its trunk grows, and that owner is generally responsible for keeping it safe. If an owner knew, or should have known, that a tree was diseased or decaying and did nothing, they could face a negligence claim. A healthy tree brought down by exceptional weather is less likely to make the owner liable. Each case depends on the facts, and this is not legal advice.

Insurance

Many home insurance policies cover damage caused by a fallen tree rather than the cost of removing the tree itself. Check your policy, keep photos and invoices, and tell your insurer.

On the road or council land

Report it to the council. If it is causing immediate danger on a road, call 999. See emergency tree work.
