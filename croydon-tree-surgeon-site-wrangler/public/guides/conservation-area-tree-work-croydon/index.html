Tree Work in a Croydon Conservation Area

Trees in a conservation area are protected even without a TPO. Here is what Croydon Council says you must do first.

The rule

Croydon Council says you must give the council six weeks' written notice before carrying out work to trees in a conservation area. It says a fine of up to £20,000 can be imposed for destroying or cutting down a tree there without consent, and up to £2,500 for pruning one without consent. I found no mention of a small-tree exemption on Croydon's page, so do not assume one applies.

Where the areas are

Our area pages cover those we have checked, including the Croham Manor Road, East India Estate, Norbury Estate, Webb Estate and Upper Woodcote Village and Kenley Aerodrome conservation areas, and those in Upper Norwood. Boundaries change, so check the council's current map.

Areas the council is considering

Croydon Council has named nine areas it would consider for conservation area status, including Croham Park Estate, Court Avenue in Coulsdon, Sanderstead Village and the Whitgift Estate, and its Cabinet agreed a list of potential new areas in December 2025. Until an area is designated the rule does not apply to it.

Emergencies

Other councils say there is an emergency exemption, but Croydon's page does not say so. Do not rely on one without checking with the council.
