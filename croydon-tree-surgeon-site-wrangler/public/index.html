Your questions answered

How is this different from a directory or marketplace?

Directories list many firms and let you collect quotes. We pass your enquiry to one local, insured tree surgeon, and our pages explain Croydon Council's tree rules from the council's own wording, so you know what applies before you commit.

Do I need permission to cut down a tree in Croydon?

Only if the tree is protected by a tree preservation order or stands in a conservation area. Croydon Council says you must give six weeks' written notice before work to a tree in a conservation area, and a fine of up to £20,000 can follow for cutting down a protected tree without permission. We check both before quoting.

What does the calculator do?

It takes a few details about the tree, the access and where you are, then shows how involved the job is, what will shape the quote and which rules to check. It never asks for a card and does not commit you to anything.

How much does tree work cost in Croydon?

It depends on the tree, the access and the work, so a firm price comes from a written quote after the tree has been seen. See our cost guide for what drives the price.

Do you cover Warlingham, Crystal Palace and Norbury?

We cover the Croydon side of Crystal Palace, Upper Norwood and Norbury, and Warlingham in Surrey. Each of those lies partly or wholly under another council, so we check which council applies to your address first.

What if the tree is dangerous right now?

If anyone is in danger, call 999. If power lines are down, call 105. Then call us and we will make the tree safe, or tell you honestly what can wait. See emergency tree work.
