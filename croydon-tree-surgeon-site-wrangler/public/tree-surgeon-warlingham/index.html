Trees and rules in Warlingham: it is Tandridge, not Croydon

Which council?

Warlingham's local planning authority is Tandridge District Council. Croydon Council's tree rules, forms and fines do not apply here. Tandridge publishes its tree preservation orders as open data, and the register includes Warlingham roads.

Tandridge says a tree preservation order is made to protect specific trees, groups of trees or woodlands in the interests of amenity, and that it prohibits cutting down, topping, lopping, uprooting, wilful damage and wilful destruction of the trees without the authority's written consent. Consent can carry conditions. Tandridge also notes that, in the Secretary of State's view, cutting roots is a prohibited activity that needs consent. Nationally, working on a protected tree without permission can lead to prosecution, with fines of up to £20,000 in a magistrates' court. Where a tree is in a conservation area but not under a TPO, councils generally require six weeks' written notice. I did not find a conservation area covering Warlingham, so check Tandridge's list; do not assume one applies. Email Tandridge's planning team or send us your address and we will check.

Common questions

Which council covers Warlingham for tree rules?

Tandridge District Council, not Croydon. Warlingham's tree preservation orders are published in Tandridge's register.

What does a TPO stop me doing?

Tandridge says a TPO prohibits cutting down, topping, lopping, uprooting, wilful damage and wilful destruction without written consent, and that cutting roots also needs consent.

Which roads have orders?

They include Westhall Road, Limpsfield Road, Farleigh Road, Crewes Lane, Homefield Road, Searchwood Road and the former Warlingham Park Hospital site on Harrow Road. Check your own tree with the council.
