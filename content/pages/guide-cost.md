**How that compares nationally.** Independent price guides put small removals at roughly £150 to £500, medium at £400 to £1,500 and large at £650 to £3,000 or more, with London running about a quarter above the rest of the country. Croydon's figures sit inside those ranges.


## What drives the price

A tree removal is priced mostly on crew time, equipment and disposal. National guides put a tree surgeon's crew at roughly £400 to £700 a day, so a job that fills a day or more cannot be cheap. The things that stretch the time:

- **Size and height.** More wood to lower, cut and carry.
- **Access.** Can we reach the tree with the chipper and vehicle, or must everything go through the house? A side gate that is too narrow means carrying every log by hand.
- **What is underneath.** A tree over a conservatory, shed, greenhouse or cables must be dismantled in small pieces and lowered on ropes. That takes far longer than dropping it.
- **Condition.** Dead, diseased or brittle trees, such as ash with dieback, need extra safety measures. See [ash dieback in Croydon](/guides/ash-dieback-croydon/).
- **Species.** Dense hardwoods like oak are slower to cut than a conifer of the same size.
- **Waste.** A big removal produces a lot. Whether we take it, chip it for you or leave logs changes the price.
- **Protection.** A TPO or conservation area adds preparation time, although Croydon charges no application fee.
- **Parking.** In controlled parking zones a bay suspension may be needed, with at least 10 working days' notice and payment first.
- **Urgency.** Emergency work costs more because it displaces booked jobs.

**Croydon makes it vary by area:**

- **Northern terraces** such as Addiscombe, Thornton Heath and Norbury have narrow gardens and little vehicle access.
- **Southern hillsides** such as Purley, Kenley, Coulsdon and Sanderstead have slopes and large mature trees.
- **Wooded edges** can mean nest and bat checks that delay work.


## Three worked examples

These are illustrative ranges built from the guide prices above, not quotes.

**1. A 5 m tree in an Addiscombe terrace garden, no side access.**
Everything goes through the house. Expect roughly £300 to £600, plus £120 or more if you want the stump ground out. If the street is in the East India Estate Conservation Area, add six weeks for the council notice, which we prepare. See [Addiscombe](/tree-surgeon-addiscombe/).

**2. A 12 m oak in a sloping Purley garden with clear access.**
A large removal with clear access is typically £1,000 to £3,000+, depending on the dismantling. A crown reduction at £800 to £1,500+ may be the better answer if the tree is healthy, protected or valuable. See [Purley](/tree-surgeon-purley/).

**3. A 10 m sycamore overhanging a conservatory in Norbury.**
This falls in the top band, £1,100 to £3,900+, because it has to come down in sections over a building and may need a platform. See [trees near buildings](/trees-near-buildings-croydon/).


## What's included and what's extra {chip=What's included}

**Usually included in a Croydon quote:**

- The crew and equipment for the job.
- Cutting down the tree and clearing the branches and brash.
- Taking the waste away and disposing of it legally, unless you keep the logs or chippings.
- The TPO and conservation area check.

**Often extra:**

- **Stump grinding,** priced mainly by diameter, £120 to £700. Doing it in the same visit saves a second call-out. See [stump grinding](/stump-grinding-croydon/).
- **A crane or platform** for large or hard-to-reach trees.
- **Parking suspension fees** charged by the council.
- **Tree reports** for an insurer, engineer or planning application. See [tree surveys](/tree-surveys-croydon/).
- **Replacement planting,** if the council requires it or you want it.

Ask whether VAT is included, because the written quote should state it either way.


## Cheaper alternatives to removal

Not every tree needs to come down. A healthy tree that is simply too big, too dark or too close to the house may only need:

- **Crown reduction,** £800 to £1,500+ for a large mature tree.
- **Crown lift or thin,** £250 to £500 for a medium tree.
- **Light prune or deadwood removal,** £200 to £300 for a small one.

A reduction can cost more than removing a small tree, but for a large, valuable or protected tree it often costs far less than the removal, the stump and the replacement. See [tree pruning](/tree-pruning-croydon/).


## Ways to pay less

- **Combine jobs.** Removal, stump grinding and a hedge cut in one visit save on call-outs.
- **Keep the logs and chippings.** Less waste to take away.
- **Clear access.** Move cars, bins and garden furniture and unlock the side gate.
- **Send photos.** We can often give a near-final price before a visit.
- **Share a visit.** Neighbours on the same street can book together.
- **Book in autumn or winter.** Many guides say these months are quieter, and winter suits most pruning. Nesting season also matters. See the [nesting season guide](/guides/bird-nesting-season-tree-work/).
- **Act early.** A dead or dying tree costs more once it is brittle.


## How to compare quotes

Get two or three for anything beyond a small job, and compare like for like. A good written quote:

- Describes the work tree by tree.
- Says whether waste is taken away and whether logs are kept.
- States what happens to the stump.
- Gives a total price and says whether VAT is included.
- Says who handles any TPO or conservation area paperwork.
- Names the insurer and cover, so ask to see the certificate.
- Gives dates and how long it will take.

**Red flags:**

- A cold caller offering same-day work.
- Cash only, with no written quote or receipt.
- A price far below the others, which often leaves out waste, insurance or proper climbing kit, or means topping the tree.
- No questions about protection. See [how to choose a tree surgeon](/guides/how-to-choose-a-tree-surgeon/).
- Pressure to decide on the spot.


## The cost of getting it wrong

- **Fines:** up to £20,000 for felling a protected tree without consent and up to £2,500 for pruning. See [tree preservation orders](/guides/tree-preservation-orders-croydon/).
- **Conservation areas:** six weeks' notice is required first. See [conservation area tree work](/guides/conservation-area-tree-work-croydon/).
- **Insurance:** home insurance can cover damage a tree has caused, and subsidence is commonly covered subject to an excess, but routine removal usually is not. Get your insurer's and an engineer's advice before felling a tree you think is affecting the house.
- **Doing it yourself:** an unqualified felling job is the most dangerous way to save money.

### How much does it cost to remove a tree in Croydon?
Most jobs run £250 to £1,100, and large trees with clear access £1,000 to £3,000 or more.

Different access assumptions, whether waste and the stump are included, insurance, and whether the cheaper quote means topping rather than removing. Compare line by line.

£120 to £700 per stump, priced mainly by diameter, with several stumps costing less each.

### Is the stump included in the tree removal price?
Usually not. It is a separate line, cheaper if done in the same visit.

### Is waste removal included?
Normally yes, unless you keep the logs or chippings. Check the quote.

### Does the price include VAT?
The written quote should say. Ask before you accept.

£800 to £1,500 or more for a large mature tree, and £250 to £500 for a lift or thin on a medium one.

### Do I pay the council to remove a protected tree?
Croydon charges no application fee for tree work consent or conservation area notice. You pay your tree surgeon.

### When is tree removal cheapest?
Autumn and winter are often quieter. Emergency work is always dearer.

From £260 for a single tree.

Yes. Crane or platform work is priced on top for large or hard-to-reach trees.
