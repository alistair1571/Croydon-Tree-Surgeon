## 1. Who We Are

This website is run by {{brand}}. We are the "data controller" for the personal information described in this policy, which means we decide how and why it is used.

**Contact for privacy matters:** use our [contact page](/contact/) or call {{phone}}.

## 2. What Information We Collect

| What we collect | When | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| Contact details | You fill in a quote form, use the calculator, call or email us | Name, phone number, email address, postcode |
| Job details | You tell us about your tree work | A description of the tree, where it is, what work you want, and anything about access, neighbours or buildings |
| Calculator inputs | You use our calculator | Job type, tree size, access, what's nearby, your area and postcode, and a summary of the results |
| Messages and photos | You email or message us, or send photos | Images of trees, gardens and buildings, which can include people, property and location data in the file |
| Call and quote records | We quote for or carry out work | Notes of calls, quotes, invoices, payment records and any council applications we prepare for you |
| Technical information | You visit the website | Your IP address and basic request information, which our hosting provider handles to deliver the site |

We don't ask for sensitive information such as health data, and ask that you don't include any in your messages or photos.

## 3. How and Why We Use It

| What we do | Lawful basis |
|---|---|
| Respond to your enquiry and provide quotes | Taking steps at your request before a contract |
| Check whether your tree is protected and prepare any council application or notice | Performing a contract, and our legitimate interests in working lawfully |
| Carry out the work, schedule visits, take payment and keep records | Performing a contract |
| Keep accounting and tax records | Legal obligation |
| Keep the site secure and prevent misuse | Legitimate interests |
| Deal with complaints, claims or disputes | Legitimate interests, and legal claims |

We don't make decisions about you using fully automated processing. The calculator gives a guide based on the answers you enter, and a person confirms every quote. We don't use your details for marketing unless you ask us to.

## 4. Who Handles Your Information

We don't sell your personal information. These are the providers that handle it on our behalf, because of how this website works:

| Provider | What they do |
|---|---|
| Formspree | Receives the quote form and calculator submissions and sends them to us by email |
| Cloudflare | Hosts and delivers this website |
| Google Fonts | Supplies the website's typeface. Your browser fetches it from Google, which receives your IP address when it does |
| Email, phone and accounting providers | Used to respond to you and keep records |

We also share information where we need to: with Croydon Council, only with your agreement, to prepare a tree works application or notice; with professional advisers and insurers; with authorities if the law requires it; and with a buyer if the business is sold.

Some of these providers may process information outside the UK, including in the United States. Where that happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Addendum.

## 5. How Long We Keep It

| Information | How long |
|---|---|
| Enquiries that don't lead to a job | 12 months, then deleted |
| Quotes and job records | 6 years after the job ends, for tax, accounting and legal claims |
| Council applications and tree reports | 6 years, or longer if a claim or dispute is ongoing |

We delete or anonymise information when we no longer need it.

## 6. Your Rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to be informed about how we use your data; to access a copy of it; to correct inaccurate information; to ask us to erase it or restrict its use in certain circumstances; to object to us using it based on legitimate interests; and, where it applies, to data portability. To use any of these rights, contact us at the address above. We'll respond within one month, and we may need to confirm your identity. There's normally no charge.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please tell us first. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

## 7. Cookies

This site doesn't set cookies of its own. If we add analytics or advertising later, we'll update this section and ask for your consent first.

## 8. Photos You Send Us

If you send photos, please avoid including people, vehicle number plates or other personal details where you can. Photos may contain location data. We use them only to assess and quote your job and as part of any council application you've asked us to make, and we don't publish them without your permission.

## 9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information. No online system is completely secure, so please take care what you send. If there's a data breach likely to put you at risk, we'll tell you and, where required, the ICO.

## 10. Children

This website is for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

## 11. Links to Other Sites

Our pages link to external sites such as council and government pages. We aren't responsible for their privacy practices.

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date shows the latest version.
