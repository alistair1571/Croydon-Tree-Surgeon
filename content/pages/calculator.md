## What Changes the Price?

| Factor | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Type of work | Light pruning costs far less than removal or a crown reduction |
| Tree size and height | Bigger trees need more people, time and equipment |
| Access | Narrow gates, steps, no side access or slopes mean more carrying and smaller machines |
| What is below and beside the tree | Roofs, glass, cables and neighbours mean rigged, sectional work |
| Crane or platform | Needed for some large trees or sites with no safe drop zone |
| Condition of the tree | Dead, diseased or storm-damaged trees can need extra safety measures |
| Number of jobs in one visit | Combining jobs, such as removing a tree and grinding the stump, is usually cheaper than booking separately |
| Waste and stump | Taking logs and chippings away adds to the job; leaving them can reduce it |
| Permissions | A TPO application or conservation area notice takes preparation; we handle it for you |
| Urgency | Emergency work costs more |
| Time of year | Late autumn and spring can be busier, so book earlier if the date is flexible |

## How to Get the Most Accurate Price {chip=Getting an accurate price}

A calculator can only go so far, because every tree and garden is different. To get an accurate fixed quote quickly:

1. **Send photos.** Take one of the whole tree from a distance, one of the trunk and base, and one of the access route (gate, passage, steps).
2. **Measure the narrowest point** of the access route.
3. **Tell us what's nearby:** buildings, glass, cables, boundaries.
4. **Say what you want done with the wood and stump.**
5. **Tell us if you know the tree is protected** or you live in a conservation area. If not, we'll check.

## Rules First, Then a Price

Before we quote, we check whether your tree is protected by a tree preservation order (TPO) or sits in a conservation area. Croydon Council says a TPO stops visually important trees being removed or pruned without formal permission, and that you must give six weeks' written notice before work to a tree in a conservation area. Fines of up to £20,000 can follow for cutting down a protected tree without permission, and up to £2,500 for pruning one. If your tree is protected, we tell you what's needed and handle the application or notice, so the quote you receive covers work you can legally do. [TPO guide](/guides/tree-preservation-orders-croydon/) and [conservation area guide](/guides/conservation-area-tree-work-croydon/).
