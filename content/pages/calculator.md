## How to get the most accurate price

The calculator gives a range because it has not seen the tree. A few details narrow it to a figure we can fix:

- **A photo of the whole tree,** taken from a few metres back, with a person or a ladder in the shot for scale.
- **A photo of the base and the route in,** including side gates, steps and any slope.
- **What is underneath or beside it:** a house, shed, conservatory, fence, cables or a road.
- **Your postcode,** so we can check for a TPO or conservation area before we price.
- **What you want done,** and why. A tree that is simply too tall may need a reduction, not removal.
- **Whether you want the stump ground and the waste taken away.**

If your road needs a parking bay suspended, Croydon asks for at least 10 working days' notice with payment first, so mention it early.


## Why two quotes for the same tree can be hundreds apart

Before you accept the lowest price, check what each quote actually covers.

- **Waste.** Is the timber and brushwood taken away, or left in a heap?
- **The stump.** Removal prices usually leave the stump behind. Grinding is priced separately.
- **Crane or platform.** Needed for some large trees, and priced on top.
- **Method.** Sectional dismantling over a house costs more than felling in the open, and for good reason.
- **Insurance.** A firm without proper cover can undercut one with it. Ask for proof, not a promise.
- **Permissions.** Does the quote include checking for a TPO or conservation area?
- **VAT.** Is the figure with or without it?
- **Parking.** Controlled parking zones can mean a bay suspension, which Croydon Council charges for.

**Ask every firm the same questions and compare like with like.** [How to choose a tree surgeon](/guides/how-to-choose-a-tree-surgeon/) lists what to check.


## What's included and what's not

**Normally included in our quotes:** the crew, equipment, cutting, clearing the branches and brushwood, and leaving the site clean.

**Not included unless the quote says so:**

- stump grinding, if you haven't asked for it
- crane or platform hire
- council fees or independent consultant reports
- work on trees that weren't on the quote

A good written quote shows the work in plain terms, what happens to the waste, whether VAT applies, and proof of insurance on request.


## What pushes a price up or down

| Goes up | Comes down |
|---|---|
| Tall or wide trees | Smaller trees |
| Tight access, slopes, steps | A clear route to the tree |
| A tree over a building or cables | An open garden |
| Dead, diseased or unstable wood | A healthy, straightforward tree |
| A crane or platform | Work done from the ground |
| Emergency or out-of-hours | Planned work |
| Waste taken away | Keeping the logs or chippings |
| One-off visit | Several jobs in one visit |

**Croydon patterns.** Terraced gardens in the north, such as Addiscombe, Thornton Heath and Norbury, often mean tight access and more hand-carrying. The southern hillsides, such as Purley, Kenley, Coulsdon and Sanderstead, tend to mean slopes and large mature trees that take more rigging. In a conservation area or for a protected tree, preparing a notice or application adds time, though Croydon Council charges no application fee.


## Permission, notices and fines

Cost isn't the only thing to check. Croydon Council says consent is needed to prune or fell a protected tree, with fines of up to £20,000 for felling and up to £2,500 for pruning. In a conservation area, six weeks' written notice is needed. We check this before we quote. See [tree preservation orders](/guides/tree-preservation-orders-croydon/) and [conservation area tree work](/guides/conservation-area-tree-work-croydon/).


## Seven ways to keep the cost down

1. Send clear photos with something for scale, so we can quote without a visit.
2. Ask whether pruning would solve it before you pay for removal.
3. Group jobs into one visit.
4. Keep the logs, or the chippings, if you can use them.
5. Clear the route to the tree beforehand.
6. Ask about timing. Quieter months can be easier to book.
7. Don't pay for a stump grind if you plan to leave it.

:::faq Tree surgery cost Croydon: frequently asked questions
### How much does tree surgery cost in Croydon?
Most garden jobs fall between about £200 and £1,100. Small removals start around £250, and a large tree over a building can pass £3,900.

### Is the calculator a quote?
No, it is a guide range. A fixed written quote follows once we have seen photos or visited.

### How much does it cost to remove a tree in Croydon?
About £250 to £450 for a small tree and £300 to £600 for a medium one. Medium-large trees run £550 to £1,100, and large trees over 9 m £1,000 to £3,900+.

### How much does stump grinding cost?
£120 to £700 per stump, depending mainly on the diameter. Several stumps in one visit cost less each.

### Is stump grinding included in tree removal?
Not unless the quote says so. Ask for it to be listed.

### How much does tree pruning cost?
About £200 to £300 for a light prune of a small tree, and £250 to £500 for a medium tree. A large mature tree can run £800 to £1,500 or more.

### Why is one quote so much cheaper than the others?
Often the waste, the stump, insurance or permissions have been left out. Compare what each quote includes.

### Can you give me a price from photos?
Yes. Send photos with the postcode and we give you a range, then a fixed quote.

### Do you charge for quotes and site visits?
No. Quotes and site visits are free.

### Why might the final quote differ from the calculator?
The calculator has not seen the tree. Access, what is underneath, the tree's condition and whether a protection check is needed can move the price, so we confirm those before we fix the quote.

### Is it cheaper to reduce a tree than remove it?
Not always. A reduction on a large mature tree costs £800 to £1,500 or more, which can be more than removing a small one. For a large, healthy or protected tree it is often cheaper than the removal, the stump and replacement.

### Is tree work cheaper in winter?
Often. Autumn and winter tend to be quieter, and suit most pruning, but nesting season and the six-week conservation area notice can also shape the timing. See the [nesting season guide](/guides/bird-nesting-season-tree-work/).

### Do prices include VAT?
Your written quote states whether VAT applies, so you can compare like with like.

### Do you cover my postcode?
We cover the London Borough of Croydon, including CR0, CR2, CR5, CR7, CR8, SE19 and SW16.

### What if my tree is protected?
We check first. Protected trees and conservation areas can need consent or notice, and cutting without it risks a fine.
