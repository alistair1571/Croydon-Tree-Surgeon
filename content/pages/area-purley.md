## Is your tree protected? Rules in Purley {chip=Is my tree protected?}

The Webb Estate and Upper Woodcote Village, both in Purley, are conservation areas. Croydon Council says you must give six weeks' written notice before carrying out work to trees in a conservation area, and the council's draft plan says there are numerous tree preservation orders across them. It also says it will resist the loss of good quality trees that make a positive contribution to the area, so removal here gets more scrutiny than pruning.

The council notes that restrictive covenants set up by William Webb may apply to individual plots and vary from plot to plot. We cannot advise on covenants; check your title documents. Brighton Road in Purley is also among the areas Croydon Council has said it would consider for conservation area status. See the [conservation area guide](/guides/conservation-area-tree-work-croydon/).

## Access, private roads and neighbours {chip=Access and parking}

The council's draft plan says most streets in the Webb Estate are private roads, some gated, owned and maintained privately by Webb Estate Limited. Access is agreed with you, and where needed with the estate, before the team arrives.

If a vehicle or chipper needs road space, Croydon Council offers a dispensation to park on yellow lines while work is carried out, or a suspension of a parking bay. It asks for at least five working days' notice, payment first and a separate licence for a skip on the road. Check current fees on the council's page.

Tell us if a tree is on a boundary or overhangs next door. You may be able to cut branches back to the boundary but not beyond it, and cuttings should be offered back. Work only goes ahead with the tree owner's consent.
