How Much Does Tree Surgery Cost in Croydon?

Tree surgery in Croydon typically costs between £200 and £1,100 for most jobs, and £1,000 to £3,000+ for large trees, depending on the type of work, the size of the tree and how easy it is to reach. The price depends on the job, not just the tree, so two trees of the same height can cost very different amounts.

How to Get the Most Accurate Price

A calculator can only go so far, because every tree and garden is different. To get an accurate fixed quote quickly:

  1. Send photos. Take one of the whole tree from a distance, one of the trunk and base, and one of the access route (gate, passage, steps).
  2. Measure the narrowest point of the access route.
  3. Tell us what's nearby: buildings, glass, cables, boundaries.
  4. Say what you want done with the wood and stump.
  5. Tell us if you know the tree is protected or you live in a conservation area. If not, we'll check.

Rules First, Then a Price

Before we quote, we check whether your tree is protected by a tree preservation order (TPO) or sits in a conservation area. Croydon Council says a TPO stops visually important trees being removed or pruned without formal permission, and that you must give six weeks' written notice before work to a tree in a conservation area. Fines of up to £20,000 can follow for cutting down a protected tree without permission, and up to £2,500 for pruning one. If your tree is protected, we tell you what's needed and handle the application or notice, so the quote you receive covers work you can legally do. TPO guide and conservation area guide.

Calculator FAQs

How accurate is the tree surgery cost calculator?

It gives a realistic guide price based on the job type, tree size, access and what's near the tree. The final price is confirmed in a fixed written quote once we've seen the job, and it can move up or down if the site differs from what you described.

Is the estimate a quote?

No. It's a guide price. A quote is a fixed, itemised price in writing, after we've seen the tree or your photos.

Is the calculator free? Do I have to book?

Yes, it's free, and there's no obligation to book.

Do your prices include VAT?

Do you charge for a site visit?

How much does it cost to remove a tree in Croydon?

Typically between £250 and £3,000+, depending on size, access and what's near the tree. See tree removal.

How much does stump grinding cost?

How much does tree pruning cost?

Why do some tree surgeons quote much less?

Check what's included. A low price may leave out waste removal, insurance or qualified staff, or may involve unsafe practices such as topping. Always ask for an itemised quote and proof of insurance and qualifications. See how to choose a tree surgeon.

Does the estimate include permission from the council?

If your tree is protected, we handle the application or notice for you. Council fees, if any, and consultants' reports aren't included unless we say so.

Can I get a price without a site visit?

Often yes for straightforward jobs, from photos. For anything over buildings, near cables or protected, we'll want to see the tree.

What if I'm not sure what I need?

Choose "Not sure" and describe the problem. We'll tell you honestly whether you need pruning, removal or a survey, and we'll say so if the cheapest option is to leave the tree alone.
