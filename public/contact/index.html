What to Send Us

A few details let us give you an accurate quote quickly:

  1. Your address and postcode. We check tree preservation orders and conservation areas for your address.
  2. What you want done: removal, pruning, stump grinding, hedge work, a survey or emergency work.
  3. Photos. One of the whole tree from a distance, one of the trunk and base, and one of the access route (gate, passage, steps).
  4. Access. The narrowest point between the road and the tree.
  5. What's nearby: buildings, glass, cables, boundaries.
  6. Anything you know about protection, such as a TPO or living in a conservation area. If not, we'll check.

What Happens Next

  1. We get back to you to talk through the job. We aim to reply the same working day.
  2. We check protection and any council notice or consent that's needed.
  3. Free site visit and written quote. We see the job, agree the method and send a fixed, itemised price. No obligation.
  4. You decide. If you go ahead, we agree a date, timing, access and what to move.
