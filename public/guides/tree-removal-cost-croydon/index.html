How Much Does Tree Work Cost in Croydon?

Most tree work in Croydon is priced by the job, not the tree. This guide gives guide prices for every job, explains what moves them, and shows you how to compare quotes properly, so you know whether the price you've been given is fair.

What You're Actually Paying For

A tree job isn't priced by the tree. It's priced by the time, the people, the equipment and the risk.

Part of the priceWhat it covers
LabourA climber and groundworkers for as long as the job takes. Most jobs need a team, not one person
EquipmentChainsaws, ropes and rigging, chipper, vehicle, and where needed a platform or crane
WasteChipping, loading and taking away branches and timber, and disposing of them legally
Insurance and compliancePublic liability cover, training, safety kit, and waste carrier registration
Planning and paperworkSite visit, quote, checking TPOs and conservation areas, and preparing any notice or application
Travel, parking and set-upGetting people and kit to site, and parking arrangements where needed

Why Is There a Minimum Charge?

Even a small job needs a team, a vehicle and equipment on site, plus travel and set-up time. In London a minimum charge is common, and it applies even to a small job. Ours is £120 to £250, depending on the job. Combining jobs in one visit is the easiest way to get more for the minimum.

Tree Removal, Pruning, Stumps and Hedges

Tree Removal

Tree removal is priced by size, access and what's around the tree. A small tree with clear access is a half-day job. A large tree in a tight garden or over a house needs more people, rigging and time. See tree removal.

Tree Pruning

Crown reduction is typically the most expensive form of pruning because it takes the most skill and time. We don't top trees. See tree pruning.

Stump Grinding

Stump grinding is priced mainly by diameter, with access as the main add-on. Several stumps in one visit usually cost less each. See stump grinding.

Hedge Cutting

Hedge cutting is priced by length, height, width and condition. Waste is a big part of the cost on conifer hedges. See hedge cutting.

Emergency Work and Surveys

Emergency work costs more, because it needs a team at short notice. Tree surveys are priced by purpose, the number of trees and the level of reporting. See emergency tree work and tree surveys.

What Should a Quote Include?

A good quote is in writing and itemised. Check it covers:

CheckWhy it matters
The work, tree by treeSo you know what's included and can compare
Waste removalLogs, branches and chippings: removed, left, or priced separately?
StumpGround out, left, or priced separately?
Total price, and whether VAT is includedNo surprises
PermissionsWho checks and applies for TPO or conservation area consent
Insurance detailsInsurer and cover
Dates and durationWhen, and how long
Payment termsDeposit and balance

Get at least two or three quotes for anything beyond a small job, and compare what's included, not just the price.

Red Flags

  • Cold callers at your door offering to cut your tree today
  • Cash only, with no written quote, receipt or insurance
  • A price far below everyone else's. Check what it leaves out (waste, insurance, qualified staff), or whether the plan involves topping the tree
  • No questions about protection. A good tree surgeon asks about TPOs and conservation areas before quoting
  • Pressure to decide immediately
  • Vague descriptions such as "tidy up the tree"

Cost FAQs

How much does a tree surgeon cost in Croydon?

Most jobs cost between £200 and £1,100, with large trees from £1,000 to £3,000+, depending on the work, the tree and the access. You get a fixed written quote after we've assessed the job.

How much does tree removal cost in Croydon?

Typically £250 to £3,000+. Small trees with easy access cost the least, and large trees in tight gardens or over buildings the most.

How much does stump grinding cost?

Typically £120 to £700 per stump, depending on diameter, depth and access. Several stumps in one visit cost less each.

Why is there a minimum charge?

Even a small job needs a team, vehicle and equipment on site, plus travel and set-up. In London a minimum charge is common.

Is the calculator price what I'll pay?

It's a guide, and the final price is a fixed written quote after we've seen the job.

Do prices include VAT?

We state whether VAT applies on every written quote, so there are no surprises.

Does the price include taking the waste away?

Normally yes, unless you ask to keep logs or woodchip. Check your quote.

Why is one quote much cheaper than another?

Check what each includes. A low price may leave out waste removal, insurance or qualified staff, or involve unsafe practices such as topping.

Does it cost anything to apply for a TPO or conservation area notice?

The council states there is currently no application fee. There may be a cost if we or a consultant prepare it.

Will my insurance pay?

It may cover damage a tree has caused, and subsidence is commonly covered, but routine removal isn't. Check your policy.

Is it cheaper to remove a tree in winter?

Often, because demand is lower, and winter is the best time for most pruning. Emergency work is always dearer.
